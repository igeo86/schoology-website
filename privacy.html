Privacy policy

Schoology Downloader · Updated September 14, 2026

Purpose and data used

This personal application copies school attachments that the account owner is authorized to access from Schoology to the owner’s Google Drive. It processes file contents, filenames, course and folder labels, attachment addresses, file hashes, and transfer status.

Google Drive access

The application uses Google OAuth and the drive.file permission through rclone. Google Drive access is used to create and manage the school-file copies and destination folders associated with this integration. It does not request permission to read Gmail or Google Contacts.

Storage and sharing

Schoology session information and Google authorization tokens are stored locally on the owner’s Raspberry Pi. Downloaded files and operational logs are also stored on that Pi; uploaded copies are stored in Google Drive. Files are retained until the owner deletes them. The application does not automatically publish these files. Any sharing is controlled by the owner’s Google Drive settings.

This tool does not sell data, use it for advertising, or send it to AI services for model training. Data is transmitted to Schoology and Google as required to retrieve and store the requested files.

Control and removal

The owner can stop the downloader, revoke its Google connection in Google Account settings, and delete the local credentials, downloaded files, and Google Drive copies. Revoking access does not itself delete existing copies.

This website

This site contains informational pages only, with no application login forms, analytics scripts, or advertising. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical access information under GitHub’s privacy statement.
